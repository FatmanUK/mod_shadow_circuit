# Pattern07 — "Return Pulse"

Since the whole point of this pattern is rebuilding after the Breakdown, I've made the density itself increase bar by bar: DeepBass (the simple 3-note pulse-cell, not the Dark Variation) is present from the very first bar since it's the featured "returning" element, but kick, hi-hat, and snare each re-enter progressively — kick starts sparse and fills in, hi-hat goes from silent to sparse to full 8ths, and the snare backbeat only reappears in the final bar. By the end of Pattern07 the groove has effectively rebuilt itself to full "Main Thump B" density, setting up the handoff into Loop Bridge and back to the top.

3. Does the snare only landing in the final bar feel like the last piece clicking into place, setting up momentum into Loop Bridge?
4. By the end of the pattern, does it genuinely feel like it's arrived back at "Main Thump B" energy — ready to hand off?
