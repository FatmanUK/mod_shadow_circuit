# Pattern02 — "Dark Variation"

Kick, hi-hat groove, and snare backbeat stay exactly as in Pattern01 (per the role map: "keep kick/hi-hat/snare groove consistent"). The change is entirely in the DeepBass line: instead of the 3-note E–D–C cell, it now runs a full **chromatic descent** (E–D#–D–C#–C) each bar — same register, more tension, same underlying shadow.

New effect introduced: **`3xx` — Tone Portamento**. Instead of retriggering the sample on a new note, the engine glides the currently-sounding pitch toward the note written in that row, at a rate of `xx` per tick (00 reuses whatever rate was last used on that channel). Used once here, at the very end of the pattern, to slide from D-3 down into C-3 rather than picking the note — a "dark drop" leading into Pattern03.

Notes on the last two rows: at `3C` the DeepBass row has **no sample number** — that's intentional (leaving it blank/unchanged is what tells ProTracker "don't retrigger, just glide toward this note"). At `3E` the effect repeats with param `00`, meaning "reuse the same portamento speed as before" rather than restating `10`.

**Audition criteria:**
1. Does the chromatic descent (E–D#–D–C#–C) read clearly as a variation of the Pattern00/01 cell — same shadow, more unease — rather than a completely different idea?

3. Does the final portamento slide (row `3C`→`3E`) actually sound like a glide/bend rather than a glitch or stuck note?
4. Does the groove (kick/hi-hat/snare) still feel identical to Pattern01, confirming it's really the bass line carrying the "darker" character here?
