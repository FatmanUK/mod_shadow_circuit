# Pattern06 — "Breakdown"

The held breath. Kick, hi-hat, and DeepBass drop out completely; only the atmosphere remains. Sweep holds a quiet sustained drone (Ch2), and NightMare (Ch4) is fully exposed, opening slightly above its usual register and then slowly sinking for almost the entire pattern — dread deepening rather than resolving.

New effect: **`2xx` — Portamento Down**. The mirror of `1xx` — continuously *lowers* pitch (raises the period) by `x` per tick, no destination note. Used here as the exact inverse of the Siren's rise in Pattern04: where that one climbed, this one sinks.That confirms it: at rate `01` over the full pattern, NightMare drifts from F-4 down to roughly D#3 by the end — a continuous, unbroken sink of a bit over an octave, never hitting a hard floor, so it's still audibly moving right up to the pattern boundary.**Pattern06 — full listing:**

- **Row `00`:** `Ch4: F-4 05 ...` (NightMare trigger only)
- **Row `01`:** `Ch2: C-4 09 ...` (Sweep trigger only)
- **Rows `02` through `3F`:** every single row carries `Ch4: ... .. 201` (the continuous descent) — nothing else, on any channel, anywhere else in the pattern.

That's the entire pattern: two triggers, then 62 rows of nothing but a slowly sinking pad over a quiet held drone. Ch1 and Ch3 are silent throughout.
