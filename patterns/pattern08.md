# Pattern08 — "Loop Bridge"

The mirror image of Pattern07's build-up: instead of rebuilding, this one winds the groove back down, bar by bar, until it's left with just the same sparse kick+bass texture Pattern00 opens with. That's the actual mechanism of a clean loop — not a hard cut from full density into the quiet seed, but a controlled descent that arrives at nearly the same texture Pattern00 starts with, so the loop point feels inevitable rather than jarring.

- **Bar 1:** full "Main Thump B" groove, continuing straight from where Pattern07 left off.
- **Bar 2:** snare drops out.
- **Bar 3:** hi-hat thins to just two sparse hits (echoing Pattern00's original hi-hat).
- **Bar 4:** only a single kick hit (the downbeat) and the bass cell remain — the same texture Pattern00 itself opens with, before its own hi-hat and atmosphere join back in.

**Audition criteria:**
1. Does bar 1 pick up naturally from where Pattern07 ended, at full groove?
2. Does each bar's thinning (snare, then hi-hat) read as a deliberate wind-down rather than parts randomly dropping out?
3. Most importantly: **does the loop point itself work** — playing straight from the end of Pattern08 into the start of Pattern00, does the kick+NightMare re-entry feel like a satisfying return rather than a jarring restart?
