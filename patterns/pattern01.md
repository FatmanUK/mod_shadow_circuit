# Pattern01 — "Main Thump B"

Design: kick and the DeepBass pulse-cell stay **identical** to Pattern00 (the seed recurring, recognizable), while the hi-hat doubles up to straight 8ths and Snare3 enters on the backbeat (beats 2 & 4) — this is the pattern where the "groove" actually locks in.

New effect introduced here: **`Cxx` — Set Volume**. What changed from Pattern00:
- **Ch1 (kick)** — unchanged, same steady quarter-note thump.
- **Ch2 (DeepBass)** — the exact same pulse-cell as Pattern00, note-for-note. It's meant to be recognized returning, not varied yet.
- **Ch3 (CloseHiHat)** — now full straight 8ths (32 hits instead of 8), alternating accented (`C38` = volume 56) and unaccented (`C20` = volume 32) via the new `Cxx` command — this is what turns the sparse "ticking" into an actual driving groove.
- **Ch4 (Snare3)** — enters for the first time, hitting beats 2 and 4 of every bar (rows 4 & 12 within each 16-row bar), landing on top of the kick to complete a classic four-on-the-floor-plus-backbeat groove.

**Audition criteria:**
1. Does the hi-hat now read as a continuous, driving 8th-note groove rather than isolated ticks — and is the loud/soft `C38`/`C20` alternation audible as a subtle rocking motion, not a jarring volume jump?
2. Does the snare's entrance on beats 2 & 4 feel like the groove "locking in," landing cleanly with the kick rather than clashing?
3. Is the DeepBass cell still clearly recognizable as the same figure from Pattern00, now just sitting inside a busier rhythm bed?
4. Overall: does this feel like a natural next step from the seed — same DNA, fuller groove — without yet feeling like a climax (that's reserved for Peak Thump later)?
